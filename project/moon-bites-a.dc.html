Questions

Before you ask for a quote

How far in advance should I book?+

Two weeks is comfortable for most events. We can turn around smaller orders in 72 hours, and we hold peak dates (December, graduation season) further out.

How much does it cost?+

Every event is quoted individually — the guest count, the menu and the setup change the number too much for a price list to be honest. Send us the details and you get a written quote within 24 hours.

Do you handle allergies and dietary needs?+

Yes. Gluten-free, nut-free, vegetarian and vegan options are available across the menu. Tell us on the form and we build the table around it.

Where do you deliver?+

Across Texas. Delivery inside the metro area is included; longer distances are quoted with the event.

Do you set up at the venue?+

Yes — styling, stands, linens and pickup are part of the service for full tables. Snack boxes are drop-off only.
